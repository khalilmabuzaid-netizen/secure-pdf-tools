Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free client-side Excel to PDF converter.

Are my financial spreadsheets uploaded to any remote server?

Never. All workbook parsing and PDF document generation occur 100% locally in your web browser memory using SheetJS and jsPDF. Your confidential numbers, salaries, and customer records never leave your computer.

Can I convert workbooks with multiple worksheets?

Yes! The tool automatically detects all sheets in your Excel workbook. You can switch between sheet tabs to preview and convert individual sheets or compile all sheets into a single document.

Will wide spreadsheets fit properly onto PDF pages?

Yes. Landscape orientation is enabled by default to provide maximum width for wide data columns, and our layout algorithm automatically scales columns and wraps text across multi-page tables.

Which spreadsheet file formats are supported?

We support modern Microsoft Excel files (.xlsx), legacy Excel spreadsheets (.xls), and standard comma-separated text files (.csv).

Are table headers repeated on every page?

Yes, by default the 'Repeat header row on each page' setting is enabled, ensuring multi-page accounting ledgers and reports remain easy to read.

Is this service free to use without limits?

Yes! The converter is 100% free with unlimited document conversions, no account sign-ups, and zero watermarks added to your files.
