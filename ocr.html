Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents or confidential files uploaded to any server?

No. Our OCR engine operates with 100% client-side processing directly in your browser. With no server uploads, your confidential files never leave your computer, ensuring absolute privacy as part of our secure PDF tools suite.

What file formats and image types can I extract text from?

You can extract text from single or multi-page PDF documents, as well as PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP image formats. The built-in renderer preserves clarity for crisp character recognition.

Can I extract mixed English and Arabic text from the same page?

Yes! You can choose the Bilingual mode to recognize both English and Arabic script simultaneously on contracts, invoices, receipts, and research papers.
