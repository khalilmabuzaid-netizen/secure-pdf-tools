Frequently Asked Questions

Is my password or original PDF sent over the internet?

Never. Protect PDF Pro relies on 100% client-side processing using Web Cryptography. With no server uploads, your password and files never leave your device, ensuring total privacy.

Which PDF viewers can open my encrypted documents?

Your protected PDF complies with universal ISO PDF encryption standards and opens seamlessly in Adobe Acrobat, Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Apple Preview, and mobile readers.

Can you recover my PDF if I forget my password?

No. Because encryption is performed locally on your device without backdoors or master keys, forgotten passwords cannot be recovered. Be sure to remember or safely store your password.

Can I restrict copying or printing on protected PDFs?

Yes. You can toggle granular permission flags to permit or disable printing, copying text and graphics, or modifying document contents.
