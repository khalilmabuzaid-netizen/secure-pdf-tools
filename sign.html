Frequently Asked Questions

Does anyone else see my signature or contract?

Never. Sign PDF Pro operates with 100% client-side processing directly in your browser session. With no server uploads, your signature data and document content remain completely private on your own device.

Can I sign documents on my phone or tablet screen?

Yes! The signature pad is fully optimized for touchscreens and stylus pens, offering smooth stroke rendering for authentic, professional signatures on mobile and desktop.

Can I place multiple signatures or sign specific pages?

You can designate any page of your document and precisely position the signature in standard signing locations such as bottom right, bottom left, or center with adjustable scaling.
