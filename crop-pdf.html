Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PDF cropping, margins, and resolution.

Does cropping a PDF delete text or reduce quality?
No. Cropping modifies the document view boundaries (CropBox and MediaBox) so that PDF readers render only the cropped region. All underlying vector text and original high-resolution elements remain intact and completely sharp.
Can I apply the same crop to all pages of a book or report?
Yes. By selecting "Apply crop to All Pages", the exact margin proportions you configure will be applied consistently across every page in the document in a single click.
Can I crop only a single specific page?
Yes. Navigate to the desired page using the page navigation bar and select "Apply to Current Page Only" before saving.
Are my uploaded documents secure and private?
Yes, 100%. All processing runs locally inside your browser memory using PDF.js and PDF-Lib. Your files never touch external servers or cloud databases.
How do points (pt) convert to millimeters (mm)?
Standard PDF points are 1/72 of an inch (approx. 0.3528 mm). You can use our unit switcher to work effortlessly in either points or millimeters.
