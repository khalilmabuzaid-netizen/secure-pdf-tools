Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my images uploaded to any remote server during conversion?

No. PDFNetizen runs 100% client-side in your web browser. Your images and documents are processed entirely in your computer's local memory and are never uploaded to any cloud server.

Can I combine multiple JPG and PNG photos into a single PDF document?

Yes! You can upload multiple JPG, PNG, and WebP images simultaneously. The converter will merge them into a single, multi-page PDF document in the sequence you specify.

Does converting images to PDF reduce their sharpness or quality?

No. The converter embeds your images directly using high-fidelity encoding, preserving original photo resolution, fine text details, and high-DPI scan clarity.

What is the difference between "Fit to Image", "Standard A4", and "US Letter"?

"Fit to Image" creates each PDF page with the exact dimensions of the corresponding photo. "Standard A4" and "US Letter" scale images proportionally into standardized international printable page dimensions.

Are there any watermarks or branding added to the PDF?

Never. PDFNetizen produces clean, professional, watermark-free PDF files ready for business, academic, or personal distribution.

Can I use this tool on my iPhone, iPad, or Android phone?

Yes! PDFNetizen is fully responsive and functions smoothly directly inside iOS Safari, Google Chrome, Samsung Internet, and other modern mobile web browsers.
