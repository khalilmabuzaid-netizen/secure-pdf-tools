Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free, client-side PDF password removal tool.

How does client-side PDF unlocking work?

When you enter your password, our in-browser decryption engine processes the cryptographic bytes directly in your device's memory using WebAssembly. It decrypts the document streams and serializes a brand new, unencrypted PDF without sending any data to external servers.

Are my confidential files or passwords uploaded to a server?

Never. All operations happen strictly within your web browser. Your confidential files, bank statements, legal contracts, and passwords are never uploaded, logged, or viewed by anyone.

Can this tool unlock a PDF without the password?

For documents protected by strong AES-256 open/user passwords, you must provide the correct password once to allow decryption. For documents with permission restrictions (like printing or copying locks), our tool can remove them directly.

Will the unlocked PDF lose formatting or image resolution?

No. The unlocking process retains the exact original page dimensions, vector typography, embedded fonts, and high-resolution graphics with zero quality loss.

Can I use Unlock PDF on mobile devices?

Yes! Our responsive web application works seamlessly on iPhones, iPads, Android smartphones, tablets, Windows, Mac, and Linux laptops.

Is there any file size limit or cost?

No. The tool is 100% free with unlimited document processing and no watermarks added to your files.
