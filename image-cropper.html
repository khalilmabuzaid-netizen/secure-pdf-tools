Frequently Asked Questions

Are my private photos uploaded to a cloud server when cropped?

Never. Image Cropper Pro runs 100% locally in your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your computer, smartphone, or tablet.

Can I specify custom pixel dimensions when cropping?

Yes! You can manually enter any desired target width and height in pixels, with an optional aspect ratio lock to prevent distortion.

Does cropping reduce image resolution or clarity?

No. Our cropper extracts pixels from the full native resolution of your original photo using high-quality bilinear interpolation, ensuring razor-sharp clarity.

Can I crop PNG images with transparent backgrounds?

Yes! When you choose PNG or WebP as the export format, full alpha transparency is preserved exactly as in the original.
