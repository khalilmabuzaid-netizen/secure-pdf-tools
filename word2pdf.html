Frequently Asked Questions

Are my Word files uploaded to any external server?

No, never. The conversion runs 100% client-side directly within your browser session using Mammoth.js and html2pdf.js. Your confidential documents, contracts, and private letters never leave your computer.

Does this tool support Arabic right-to-left (RTL) Word documents?

Yes! Our engine automatically detects Arabic script, applying proper right-to-left layout and embedding clean Arabic typography (such as Cairo and Tahoma) so your Arabic documents look sharp and readable.

Does it preserve tables, headings, and bullet points?

Yes. Headings, bold/italic text styles, numbered and bulleted lists, and structured data tables are parsed and converted cleanly into the resulting PDF.

What Word file formats are supported?

The converter supports all standard Microsoft Word (.docx) files created in Word 2007 through Word 2024, Office 365, Google Docs, and LibreOffice.
