Frequently Asked Questions

Are my documents uploaded to a cloud server to merge?

No. Merge PDF Pro executes with 100% client-side processing in your browser. With no server uploads, your private files never touch the internet, ensuring optimal privacy and compliance.

Does merging affect the quality of text or images in my PDFs?

Not at all. The merging engine uses native vector page cloning, preserving original document formatting, sharp text, and high-resolution images without compression artifacts.

How many files can I combine in a single merge operation?

You can combine multiple documents simultaneously. The process is powered directly by your computer hardware for lightning-fast results.
