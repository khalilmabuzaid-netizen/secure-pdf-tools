Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF documents uploaded to any remote server?

No, never. The PDF to Word conversion executes 100% client-side directly within your browser's JavaScript engine. Your private files, contracts, and financial reports never touch the cloud or any third-party server.

Does this tool support Arabic and bilingual PDF documents?

Yes! Our extraction algorithm features intelligent bidirectional detection, automatically applying Right-to-Left (RTL) formatting and Arabic typography (such as Cairo or Tahoma) to Arabic paragraphs while preserving Left-to-Right English text.

Which applications can open the downloaded .docx file?

The output file is a standard OpenXML Microsoft Word document (.docx) compatible with Microsoft Word 2013-2024, Microsoft 365, Google Docs, Apple Pages, LibreOffice, and mobile Office apps.

Can I convert scanned PDFs or image-based documents?

If your PDF is a scanned image without a text layer, use our free OCR Text Extractor tool on the homepage first to recognize the text, and then export it into Word or text format with high accuracy.
