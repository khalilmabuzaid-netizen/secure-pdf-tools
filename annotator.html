Zero Cloud Risk: True On-Device PDF Editing

Most online PDF editors require you to upload sensitive contracts, tax documents, or confidential research to external servers. This creates potential privacy vulnerabilities, compliance risks, and slow upload speeds.

PDF Netizen Annotator works entirely in your browser using Mozilla PDF.js, Fabric.js, PDF-Lib, and MathJax 3. All vector calculations, pen strokes, text rendering, and PDF flattening occur locally on your machine. No documents or keystrokes ever leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to annotate confidential documents here?

Yes, absolutely. All rendering and annotation occurs 100% locally in your browser memory. Your files and annotations never touch any remote server or the internet, guaranteeing total privacy and compliance.

How do I add math formulas or symbols to my PDF?

Select the Math LaTeX tool from the dock, enter standard LaTeX syntax (such as $E = mc^2$ or calculus integrals), view the live rendered MathJax preview, and click insert to place it anywhere on your page.

Can I annotate PDFs offline without an internet connection?

Yes! PDF Netizen is designed as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). Once cached in your browser, you can annotate documents without internet access.

Is there a page count or file size limit?

No. PDF Netizen Annotator is 100% free with no arbitrary limits, no page caps, and no watermark additions. Processing speed depends only on your local device hardware.

How can I annotate a PDF document online for free?

Simply drag and drop your PDF file into the workspace, choose your markup tool (Pen, Highlighter, Text, or Math Formula), draw or annotate on your document, and click 'Download PDF' to export instantly.
