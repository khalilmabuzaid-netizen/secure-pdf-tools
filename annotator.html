Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF documents uploaded to any server while annotating?

No, never. AnnotatePDF Pro processes all files 100% locally inside your browser using PDF.js and Fabric.js. Your documents never leave your computer or mobile device.

Can I insert mathematical formulas and LaTeX equations onto my PDF?

Yes! Our built-in MathJax integration lets you type LaTeX equations (such as integrals, fractions, matrices, and symbols) and renders them as sharp vector SVG objects directly on your document.

Does annotating flatten or damage existing PDF text and links?

When you download your annotated PDF, our client-side engine embeds your drawings, text annotations, and formulas directly onto the PDF canvas without altering the underlying vector structure or content.

Can I use AnnotatePDF on mobile devices or touchscreen tablets?

Yes! The interactive canvas is fully responsive and supports touch gestures, stylus pens, and digital pencils for smooth freehand drawing and highlighting on iPads, tablets, and smartphones.
