Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free client-side PDF page organization tool.

Are my PDF documents uploaded to any server?

Never. All page thumbnail rendering and PDF re-assembly execute 100% locally in your browser memory using PDF.js and PDF-Lib. Your confidential files never leave your computer or phone.

Does organizing pages degrade quality or blur text?

No. The reordering engine copies native vector PDF page structures directly without rasterizing or re-compressing contents. All original fonts, vector graphics, forms, and image resolution remain 100% intact.

Can I restore deleted pages before downloading?

Yes! Whenever you delete a page, a 'Restore Deleted' button appears in the toolbar. You can also click 'Reset Order' to revert the entire document to its original state.

Can I reverse the entire page order with one click?

Yes! Simply click the 'Reverse' button in the toolbar to flip the entire sequence from back to front instantly.

Is there any limit on file size or number of pages?

No. The tool is 100% free with unlimited document processing and no watermarks added to your files.

Can I organize PDFs on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes! Touch-friendly drag-and-drop and responsive controls work seamlessly on iPhones, iPads, Android smartphones, tablets, Windows, Mac, and Linux.
